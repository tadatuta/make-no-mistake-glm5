# Step 1: Brainstorm Startup Ideas

**Date:** 2026-02-23
**Status:** In Progress
**Participants:** CEO, Creative Consultant (IDEO), Innovation Consultant (McKinsey), AI Product Expert

---

## Executive Summary

This document captures the brainstorming session for identifying a startup idea that meets the following criteria:
- Zero investment required (fully AI-executable)
- Viral growth potential
- Low competition / difficult to replicate
- Clear monetization path

---

## Consulting Input

### Creative Consultant (IDEO) Perspective

Key principles for viral, zero-investment products:
1. **Emotional resonance** - products that make people feel something spread naturally
2. **Social currency** - users should want to share their experience to look good
3. **Triggers** - built-in reminders that keep the product top-of-mind
4. **Practical value** - genuinely useful content gets shared

### Innovation Consultant (McKinsey) Perspective

Market gaps in AI-native products:
1. Most AI products are "AI wrappers" - thin layers over GPT
2. Underserved: emotional AI, personalized experiences, creative tools
3. Blue ocean: AI for personal transformation and self-expression
4. Trend: Gen Z values authenticity over polish

### AI Product Expert Perspective

What's technically feasible with zero investment:
1. Static web apps (HTML/CSS/JS) - hostable on GitHub Pages, Vercel, Netlify
2. Browser-based AI integration (using free API tiers)
3. Progressive Web Apps (PWA) - no app store needed
4. Content generation tools - leverage existing AI models

## Brainstormed Ideas

### Idea 1: AI Dream Interpreter & Visualizer
**Concept:** Users describe their dreams, AI interprets psychological meaning and generates visual art representation.

**Pros:**
- Highly shareable (visual content)
- Emotional engagement
- Viral potential on social media
- Zero cost (free image generation APIs)

**Cons:**
- Several competitors exist
- Limited repeat engagement

**Verdict:** ❌ Too competitive

---

### Idea 2: Personal Myth Creator
**Concept:** AI transforms user's life story into a personalized mythological narrative with hero's journey structure, complete with custom pantheon based on their values.

**Pros:**
- Unique positioning
- Deeply personal = high shareability
- Can create visual assets
- No direct competitors

**Cons:**
- Complex to execute well
- May need multiple AI integrations

**Verdict:** ⚠️ Interesting, needs validation

---

### Idea 3: "Anti-Resolution" Generator
**Concept:** Instead of traditional resolutions, users get AI-generated "anti-resolutions" - humorous, counterintuitive life advice that's actually psychologically sound. "This year, disappoint your parents less by caring less about disappointing them."

**Pros:**
- Highly viral (humor + insight)
- Simple to build
- Shareable quotes
- Counter-cultural appeal to Gen Z

**Cons:**
- May be seen as novelty
- Limited depth

**Verdict:** ⚠️ Good viral hook, but limited as standalone product

---

### Idea 4: Memory Palace Generator
**Concept:** Users input what they want to memorize (vocabulary, speeches, study material), AI creates a personalized "memory palace" - a visual, narrative journey through an imaginary space that encodes the information using ancient mnemonic techniques.

**Pros:**
- Genuine utility (learning tool)
- Unique visual output
- Shareable memory journeys
- Educational market is large
- No direct AI competitor doing this specifically

**Cons:**
- Requires explanation of concept
- May need visual generation

**Verdict:** ✅ Strong candidate

---

### Idea 5: AI "Future Self" Letter Generator
**Concept:** Users answer questions about their current life, AI generates a letter from their "future self" 10 years from now - offering perspective, wisdom, and predictions. The twist: it's psychologically designed to motivate positive change.

**Pros:**
- Emotional impact
- Highly shareable
- Simple to build
- Viral on TikTok/Instagram
- Can expand to coaching

**Cons:**
- One-time use mostly
- Several generic "future self" tools exist

**Verdict:** ⚠️ Needs differentiation

---

### Idea 6: "Your Life as a Movie" Generator
**Concept:** AI analyzes user's life story and creates a complete movie treatment: title, genre, plot summary, character posters, soundtrack suggestions, and even a "trailer script." Users can explore alternative genres for their life.

**Pros:**
- Extremely shareable
- Creative and fun
- Multiple output formats
- Viral potential high
- No direct competitor with full treatment

**Cons:**
- Complex to build all components
- Need image generation

**Verdict:** ✅ Strong candidate

---

### Idea 7: Personal Prophecy Oracle
**Concept:** Daily AI-generated "prophecies" that are actually personalized insights based on user's goals and challenges. Uses mystical/fantasy framing but delivers real value. Users can "consult the oracle" for decisions.

**Pros:**
- Habit-forming (daily engagement)
- Unique positioning
- Shareable prophecies
- Gamification potential

**Cons:**
- May seem gimmicky
- Need to deliver real value

**Verdict:** ⚠️ Interesting but risky

---

### Idea 8: Relationship Time Capsule
**Concept:** Couples/friends create AI-mediated "time capsules" - answering questions about each other, with AI generating predictions, compatibility insights, and future scenarios. Can be "opened" at set dates.

**Pros:**
- Social by design
- Emotional engagement
- Gift potential
- Unique concept

**Cons:**
- Requires two users
- Privacy concerns

**Verdict:** ⚠️ Good but complex

## Decision Matrix

### Winner: **"Your Life as a Movie" Generator**

**Rationale:**
1. **Highest viral potential** - people love sharing content about themselves
2. **Multiple shareable outputs** - title, poster, trailer script, soundtrack
3. **Emotional resonance** - seeing your life as a narrative is powerful
4. **Expandable** - can add features like "alternative endings," "sequel," "genre twist"
5. **Zero investment viable** - can use free tiers of AI APIs
6. **Clear monetization** - premium features, physical prints, expanded treatments

**Working Title:** "LifeFilm" or "MyLifeMovie" or "CineLife"
